# English manuscript

`main.tex` is the English paper draft for the fifteen equal-disk packing certificate. It summarizes the geometric reductions and the computer-assisted proof, and points to the self-contained verifier in `../candidate_bundle/`.

Build with XeLaTeX or open `main.tex` in Codex for the built-in LaTeX preview. The paper labels the result as computer-assisted and notes that independent mathematical review remains outstanding.
